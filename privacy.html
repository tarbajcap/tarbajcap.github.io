CAP Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026
Application: CAP – Grocery Flyer Price Comparison
Operator: Slavomír Tarbaj, Slovakia
Contact: tarbaj.cap@gmail.com

1. Introduction

We respect your privacy. CAP does not require user registration and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Certain technical information may be processed by third-party services used within the application, such as advertising providers and AI services, solely to provide app functionality.

2. Information We Process

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and Appodeal)

CAP displays advertisements through two advertising mediation platforms: Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and Appodeal. A mediation platform lets several advertising networks compete to show an ad.

These platforms and their partner advertising networks may process certain information, including:

  • Advertising ID (GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address
  • Device type, model, and operating system version
  • Language and country settings
  • Advertising interactions (ad views and clicks)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address

Advertising networks used through Unity LevelPlay: Unity Ads, ironSource Ads, Meta Audience Network, and InMobi. Advertising networks used through Appodeal: AppLovin, BidMachine, Vungle (Liftoff), Mintegral, BigoAds, InMobi, Amazon Ads, DT Exchange, VK Ads, and Unity Ads. These networks may process the information listed above in order to deliver and measure advertising.

CAP does not use Google AdMob.

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, CAP displays a consent form (IAB Transparency and Consent Framework) provided by Appodeal before any advertising is loaded. Your choice is passed to the advertising platforms and networks above. Personalized advertising is shown only when consent has been granted. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the application under Settings → Ad consent.

On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Your precise GPS location and your loyalty cards are never shared with advertising providers.

  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Appodeal Privacy Policy: https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy/
  • Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/
  • InMobi Privacy Policy: https://www.inmobi.com/privacy-policy

AI Assistant (Google Gemini)

When using the AI Assistant feature, the text you enter may be sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response.

Only the content you voluntarily submit is processed. CAP does not attach your name, email address, or other identifying information to these requests.

Gemini Terms and Policies: https://ai.google.dev/terms

Analytics, Crash Reporting, and Notifications (Google Firebase)

CAP uses Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics), crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics), remote configuration, and push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).

Firebase may process a device identifier, IP address, device type, and application version. This information is used solely to keep the application stable and to deliver the notifications you have enabled. CAP does not use it to identify individual users.

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Price Alerts and New Flyer Notifications

If you use price alerts (watched products or categories) or notifications about new flyers, CAP stores the following on its server (Supabase) so that it can send you these notifications even when the application is closed:

  • Push notification token of your device (issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Selected country
  • Watched products or categories and the stores you selected

This information is not linked to your name, email address, or any account. It is used only to send the notifications you requested. You can stop the notifications at any time by removing your watched items or disabling notifications for CAP in your device settings. To request deletion of this data, contact us at the email address below.

Loyalty Cards

Loyalty cards you add to CAP (store name, card name, card number, and barcode format) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to CAP servers or to any third party.

When you share a card with a family member, the card is transferred directly between devices using a QR code. No server is involved.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only to scan the barcode of a loyalty card. Camera images are processed on your device and are not stored or uploaded.

If you choose to load a card from a photo in your gallery, the barcode is read on your device. The photo is not uploaded anywhere.

Camera and photo access are requested only when you use these features, and you can revoke them at any time in your device settings.

Location (optional)

If you turn on the optional in-store card reminder, CAP uses your device location to find stores near you and offer the matching loyalty card.

To look up nearby stores, your coordinates are sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass API (overpass-api.de). The coordinates are sent without any identifier, are processed only for that single request, and are not stored by CAP or sent to CAP servers.

Location is used only while the feature is enabled. You can turn it off at any time in the application and revoke the location permission in your device settings.

OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

Backend Services (Supabase and Cloudflare)

CAP uses Supabase to deliver current product prices, flyer information, and application data.

Flyer pages are delivered through Cloudflare R2 storage. Only the flyer images themselves are stored there; no personal data is stored or processed.

Requests may include technical information necessary to provide the service, but CAP does not intentionally collect personal user information through these requests.

Local Storage

The application stores certain data locally on your device, including:

  • Selected language
  • Selected country
  • Favorite products
  • Shopping lists
  • Saved AI-generated recipes
  • Loyalty cards and their usage statistics

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to CAP servers.

Apart from the notification data described above, CAP does not store user data on its own servers. Local application data remains on the user's device until deleted by the user or by uninstalling the application.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

CAP does not intentionally collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Files stored on your device

CAP does not store your location history, your photos, or your loyalty card numbers on any server.

CAP does not use custom analytics tools for user profiling.

CAP is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We take reasonable measures to protect data processed through the application and its third-party service providers.
